Features

Everything a small business does every day

Sales, purchases, stock, money and reports in one window. Simple enough for the counter, detailed enough for your CA.

GST bills in seconds

Press F2, choose the customer, type the first letters of an item and add the quantity. Milaan fills in the rate, HSN, batch and GST, works out CGST/SGST or IGST from the place of supply, and rounds off.

  • Sales invoices, quotations, returns and credit notes
  • Purchase bills and purchase returns
  • Print, save as PDF or export to Excel (CSV)
  • Receipts, payments and who owes you what

Clean, professional invoices

Your name, address, GSTIN and bank details on every bill, with a GST summary by rate and the amount in words. Add your own logo, signature and terms.

  • Batch, expiry and MRP columns for pharmacies
  • Discounts per line, round off and balance due
  • Your own invoice numbering

Stock that keeps itself up to date

Every sale and purchase moves stock automatically. See what is running low, what is about to expire and what your stock is worth at cost.

Bank accounts, credit limits & loans

Keep cash, bank and cash-credit accounts in one place. Milaan shows how much of your bank limit is still free, and reminds you before an EMI is due or a limit needs renewing.

Installing on Windows

If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info and then Run anyway. This appears for new apps that Microsoft does not know yet.

Installing on Mac

Open the .dmg and drag Milaan ERP to Applications. The first time, right-click the app and choose Open, then Open again. If macOS still refuses, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → About This Mac. “Chip: Apple M…” means Apple chip; “Processor: Intel” means Intel.

Questions

Good to know

Where is my data kept? Is it safe?

Your books are saved on your own computer, in a “Milaan ERP Data” folder inside Documents. Nothing is uploaded to us. Milaan keeps restore points and you can make a backup file any time; copy it to a pen drive or Google Drive for extra safety.

Do I need the internet?

No. Billing, stock and reports all work offline. The internet is used only to download updates.

What happens when my plan or trial ends?

Milaan becomes read-only. You can still open, print, export and back up everything, but you cannot add or change entries until you renew. Your data is never deleted or locked away. Paid plans also get 3 days of grace after the end date to renew.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Each licence is for one computer. For a second counter or another office, buy another licence. Message us if you need several and we will work something out.

I run two businesses. Do I need two licences?

No. One licence lets you keep several firms on the same computer, each with its own books.

I am changing my computer. What do I do?

Make a backup in Milaan, install Milaan on the new computer and restore the backup. Then send us the new computer’s code on WhatsApp so we can move your licence.

How do updates work?

Milaan checks for new versions by itself and installs them when you are ready. Your data is not touched by updates.

Can I move from Marg ERP?

Yes. Export your items and parties from Marg as Excel/CSV and use Import from Marg in Milaan. We are happy to help over WhatsApp.

Does it work for my type of business?

Milaan has ready-made setups for retail, pharmacy, restaurant, apparel, electronics, hardware and service businesses, and you can rename fields and lists to fit any trade. The free trial is the best way to check.
